# Jarvis HUD (world-state main view + assemble demo) — surface brief

Scope: `apps/ze-web/src/pages/jarvis/`, routes `/jarvis` (main view, voice orb) and
`/jarvis/assemble` (the "what's slipping?" demo moment). Mode: Operate for the calm
everyday state; the cinematic state is a demo register, not a second mode. Build path:
code-led (operate surface). Fresh build; prototypes A–D are abandoned and nothing but
`pages/proto/model` data is reused (copied in as data).

Audience and job: one technical user, desktop mornings and phone through the day, who
wants to see what Ze understands and what it is doing about it, never a list of events,
tasks or notes. Success: the user reads what is slipping and why, what Ze is doing for
them right now, how sure Ze is, and what Ze wants them to decide, in one look.

Pinned by the user (2026-10-17, binding, beats the roll): heavy GMUNK / Oblivion HUD
construction; purple leads, not cyan; main view = the world-state as one framed hero
viewport with a status rail left and feeds right; answers assemble as modules; a
Nothing-style dot-matrix voice orb that breathes, listens and speaks as a waveform;
honesty in the visuals (solid told, dotted inferred/suspected, glow fades with age,
coral slipped); a calm state and a cinematic state; works at phone width.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ze is a mind, shown thinking. The screen is a HUD over one living world-state:
what Ze knows and how sure, what it watches, what slips, what it is doing, what it
wants decided. It refuses the agenda/inbox arrangement: no list of events or notes leads.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black violet field with a faint dot grid; hairline frames, bracketed
corners, edge rulers with coordinates, micro-labels in a wide geometric caps face
(Michroma), thin numerals and sentences in Saira 200–400, dot-matrix display words
drawn as real dots. Three purple-led palettes, mixed in oklab, each with one main
purple, one coral for slipped, cream text. Solid stroke = told; dotted = inferred or
suspected; glow fades with evidence age; coral = drifting.

STORY: The user arrives on the orb breathing at the center of a ring field of their
concerns, reads what slipped and why from the field, sees Ze's own status and what it
is doing on the left, the inflows and confidence on the right, and asks "what's
slipping?" to watch the answer assemble with one sentence from Ze and a decision.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): top band: route tabs (World, Assemble), palette switch,
Calm/Cinematic toggle, orb-state cycler, all standard controls. Left rail 260px: Ze
status (online, tier, quiet hours, push budget as dots), Doing now (goal, workflows,
workspace), Needs you. Center framed viewport: dot-matrix orb at center, three
concentric horizon rings (today, week, later) with concern nodes, people and projects
as satellites; Ze's one sentence below. Right rail 300px: inflows, loops with
confidence meters, while-you-were-away. Bottom: Ask Ze input with mic. Phone: one
column, controls wrap, orb+field square, rails stack as modules.

Signature interaction: the assembly. On "what's slipping?" the orb listens, then
speaks (waveform), the viewport clears and modules cascade into fixed cells: bracket
frames draw, titles type, confidence ticks fill, coral deadlines count, Ze's sentence
lands last with Draft / Snooze / Not now. Reduced motion: cells appear together,
opacity only; the orb holds a static shaded state and changes color per state.

FORM: Sci-fi film HUD (GMUNK, pinned), position 1 of 7; roll assigned 6 (split-flap)
donating the cascade topology. seed:6997123e012b1469

Honest risk: HUD fatigue in daily use; the calm state must stay glanceable, and the
world's construction must never replace standard controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
