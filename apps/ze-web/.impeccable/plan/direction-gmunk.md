The user's words: "I loved GMUNK. Thats what we will go with. HEAVY GMUNK inspiration UI." Also: "it is also important to also have the voice orb, the ambience", and "I like the nothing OS dotted stuff we could use that, both for the orb and for the... overall mood".

The goal is a demo-able, slick, "wow" UI. "I want Ze to be Jarvis."

## References
- https://gmunk.com/GM-Concept-UI
- GMUNK's Oblivion (2013) TET / Skytower UI, which the user shared as five stills: a HUD module sheet, a topographic route map, the TETVISION "DNA analysis" and "signal data / ANOTHER DAY IN PARADISE" screens, and the three-screen Skytower desk.
- Nothing OS: dot-matrix type, monochrome, one red accent.

## What the references show (from the stills)
- **Field:** a near-black blue-grey background with a faint dot grid over everything.
- **Construction:** hairline rules, bracketed corners, registration ticks, edge rulers with grid coordinates (A–G, 1–13), and small "readout" micro-labels everywhere.
- **Palette:** cyan/teal as the main color; pale cream text; coral/red-orange for alerts and active chips ("DECODE", "MAIN MAP"); red diagonal hatching for a danger zone; one amber route line on the map.
- **Type:** a wide geometric sans, all caps and letter-spaced for labels; large thin numerals for clocks and counts (00:01:15, 17:26); tiny secondary text.
- **Dot matrix:** "ANOTHER DAY IN PARADISE" is set in dot-matrix inside the HUD. This is the bridge to Nothing OS.
- **Data viz:** segmented radial rings, concentric arcs, a triangular velocity vector, tick-bar meters, a voice waveform beside a portrait, a dot helix joined by lines, topographic contours with a glowing route.
- **Layout:** stacked module rails on the left (status) and right (feeds), with one framed hero viewport in the middle. The three-screen desk shows a calm, glanceable working state.

## Mapping it to Ze (first proposal, for the user to refine)
- **Hero viewport = the world-state.** Concerns, people and projects drawn as a live field, a topography or ring system. Slipping items burn coral, and suspected items are dotted.
- **Rails = modules.** Left: Ze's status (online, quiet hours, budget, what Ze is doing now). Right: feeds (email, calendar, ingestion, loops with their confidence).
- **The voice orb = Ze's presence.** A dot-matrix particle sphere (Nothing OS) that breathes when idle, listens, and speaks with a waveform like Oblivion's "voice feed". It is the ambient center.
- **Claims honesty in the visual language:** solid means told, dotted means inferred or suspected, glow fades as evidence ages, coral means slipped.
- **Talk** happens over the HUD, not in a separate chat page. Answers assemble as modules in the viewport, like the "what's slipping?" demo moment.
- **Watch for:** HUD fatigue in daily use. Plan a calm default state, like the Skytower desk, and a cinematic state for demos and big answers. Make sure phone width still works.

## User decisions after reading the mapping (2026-10-17)
- "I like the main view idea, and components assembling. And all that you said." The mapping above is accepted as the starting point.
- "I like the purple coloring tho." Purple leads the palette, not GMUNK's cyan. Explore purple-led palettes that keep the HUD's discipline: a dark field, one main hue, a coral alert, cream text. Check contrast. Mix colors in oklab, not oklch, which shifts hue.
- "I want to abandon the prototypes and try something new with this new vision." Prototypes A to D are abandoned. Start fresh, and take nothing from them except the mock data.
